Privacy Policy for mpvEx

mpvEx (mpvExtended) is designed from the ground up to respect your digital freedom and privacy. We do not track you, we do not show advertisements, and we never collect your personal data.

Application: mpvEx (mpvExtended)
Package ID: app.marlboroadvance.mpvex
Effective Date: September 27, 2026
License: Apache 2.0
Key Highlights (At a Glance)

Your privacy is paramount. Here is what you need to know about how mpvEx handles data:

  • Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.
  • No Ads or Trackers: Completely free of advertising SDKs and tracking telemetry.
  • Local Processing: Video playback, history, and preferences stay 100% on your device.
  • 100% Open Source: Fully transparent code auditable by anyone on GitHub.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how mpvEx (mpvExtended) ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), available under package name app.marlboroadvance.mpvex, handles information when you install and use our Android media player.

mpvEx is a media player built upon the open-source libmpv engine and Material3 design principles. We believe that media playback is personal, and our software is built strictly as a client-side tool for users to play their own audio and video files.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal data or usage analytics. In particular:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account information.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not record your IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, advertising ID, or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: There are no analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, Mixpanel, etc.) embedded in the app.
  • No Advertising Networks: There are no advertising SDKs, banner ads, interstitial ads, or tracking cookies.
  • No Media Uploads: We never upload, inspect, transmit, or copy your private video files, audio files, photos, or playback history to any remote server or cloud infrastructure.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

To function as a media player on Android, mpvEx requests certain system permissions. Each permission is used exclusively for the specified feature and operates locally on your device:

Permission Type / Target Purpose & Functionality
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Media / Storage Allows the app to locate, browse, read metadata (using MediaInfo), generate thumbnails, and play video and audio files stored on your device or SD card.
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
(Standard flavor only)		 All Files Access Used in the direct distribution build to enable full file management features (renaming, moving, deleting video files, subtitle loading across arbitrary directories, and USB OTG drives). Not present or used in Scoped Storage builds.
INTERNET Network Required solely to stream network media streams initiated by you (e.g., via "Open URL", HTTP/HLS/DASH/RTSP streams), network shares (SMB/WebDAV/FTP), and to check for application updates on GitHub (in non-store builds).
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Background Service Allows uninterrupted audio and video playback when switching apps, using Picture-in-Picture (PiP), or turning off the screen, and exposes system media session controls.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Required on Android 13+ to display the active media playback notification with playback controls (play, pause, next, previous, seek bar).
REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES
(Standard flavor only)		 App Updates Allows self-updating the application when you manually choose to download an update from GitHub releases. Not used for installing third-party applications.

You can revoke any runtime permission at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > mpvEx > Permissions). Note that revoking storage permissions will prevent the app from browsing and playing local media.

4. Local Data Storage & User Data Control

All application state, user preferences, and playback metadata are stored strictly on your local device:

  • Playback History & Resumption: Information about recently played media files, playback positions, timestamps, and durations are stored locally in an on-device SQLite / Room database.
  • Application Preferences: Your configured settings (playback speed, gesture controls, subtitle styling, hardware decoding preferences, mpv config scripts) are stored locally in Android DataStore / SharedPreferences.
  • Data Deletion: You have complete control over your data. You can clear your playback history at any time from within the app settings, or clear all app data and cache via Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > mpvEx > Storage > Clear Data). Uninstalling the application removes all local app databases and preferences.

5. Network Streaming & Third-Party Servers

When you choose to play a remote URL (such as an HTTP, HTTPS, HLS, or RTSP stream), or connect to a custom network share (SMB, WebDAV, FTP):

  • mpvEx connects directly from your device to the target server you provided.
  • We operate no proxy servers, intermediary relay servers, or logging gateways. Your connection to that third-party streaming source is governed by that third party's privacy policy and terms.
  • Any authentication credentials entered for network shares (e.g., SMB/WebDAV passwords) are stored securely on your device and are never sent anywhere other than the designated host server.

6. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

mpvEx includes a local crash viewer (CrashActivity) that displays crash stack traces directly to you on screen if an unexpected error occurs.

Crash logs are never transmitted automatically. If you encounter an issue and choose to report it on our GitHub Issues page, you may manually copy and paste the log, giving you full discretion to inspect and redact any personal information or file names before sharing.

7. Children's Privacy

mpvEx is a general-purpose media player utility. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because our application does not collect any personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with regulations such as COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K.
